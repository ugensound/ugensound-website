Ugen

Ugen Sound is an innovative audio collective that specializes in crafting immersive sounds for visual media.
Their work spans sound design, foley artistry, composition and post-production, tailored to enhance storytelling
in film, animation, video games and advertising.
Ugen brings cinematic experiences to life through meticulously crafted audio.
